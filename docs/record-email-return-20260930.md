# Return to the originating customer or Lead after sending email

The global email composer retains the customer or Lead from which it was
opened. A manually entered customer recipient is sent as a customer email and
is therefore visible in that customer's email panel. Lead sends keep their
Lead identifier.

After a successful immediate or scheduled send, customer messages return to
the customer communication panel and Lead messages return to the Lead email
panel. Mailbox-origin sends continue to return to the email center.
